title: "Dark Mode Makes Text Look Bold"
description: "It's not your imagination. Light text on dark backgrounds looks heavier because of how your eyes work."
---

Ever notice text looks bolder in dark mode? It's not a rendering bug. Your eyes do this thing called halation where bright pixels bleed into surrounding dark ones, making strokes look thicker. Some sites drop the font weight to 300 in dark mode to compensate. Monospace fonts make it worse because every stroke is the same width.
